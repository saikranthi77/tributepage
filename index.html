A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a key role in India's space and missile development programs.

Achievements

  • 11th President of India
  • Known as Missile Man of India
  • Author of "Wings of Fire"
  • Bharat Ratna Award
